    Trust anchor validation

    The Trust Anchor Validation Process verifies the cryptographic integrity of a LoTE and checks its signing certificate against the set published for its type in the OJA. For QEAA Providers, it instead authenticates the pilot LOTL and the Trusted List it points to. The accepted LoTE and the accepted pilot Trusted List are the sources for Trust Anchors. A Trust Anchor is an X.509 certificate containing the name and public key used by a Wallet Unit or WRP to validate an artifact or Attestation.

    APTITUDE Implementation Choice

    All Trust Anchors SHALL be obtained from the applicable dedicated LoTE, except those of QEAA Providers, which SHALL be obtained from the pilot LOTL and Trusted Lists as specified in this section. This applies to PID Providers, Wallet Providers, Providers of WRPAC, Providers of WRPRC, PuB-EAA Providers, EAA Providers, Registrars and their Registers.

    Depending on the artifact or Attestation being verified, the validating entity SHALL fetch, download, and validate the dedicated LoTE for the required entity type, or the pilot LOTL and Trusted List for QEAA. The lists are used to retrieve Trust Anchors for validating:

    1. Infrastructure Certificates: WRPACs and WRPRCs.
    2. Wallet Unit Attestations (WUAs): Key Attestation (KA) and Wallet Instance Attestation (WIA).
    3. PID Signatures: Person Identification Data (PID).
    4. Attestation Signatures and Seals: QEAA (through the pilot TL and LOTL), EAA, and Pub-EAA.
    5. Artifacts and Attestation Status and Revocation Information: Status List Token for Attestations, KA, WIA, and WRPRC status information; CRL or OCSP for WRPAC and Sign/Seal Certificate status information.
    6. Registrar-signed Artifacts: Register information.

    APTITUDE Implementation Choice

    The Trust Anchors for EAA Providers SHALL be retrieved from and validated against their dedicated LoTE, and the same LoTE validation process SHALL be used for these Trust Anchors as for all other APTITUDE entities. The Trust Anchors for QEAA Providers SHALL be retrieved only from the pilot LOTL and Trusted List, validated under the dispatch and runtime acceptance rules of this section, and SHALL NOT fall back to a LoTE.

    To validate a retrieved LoTE under the APTITUDE profile, the validating entity SHALL:

    • obtain the location and authorized signing certificate set for the requested LoTE type from the Official Journal of APTITUDE (OJA);
    • verify the LoTE signature or seal using the format-specific procedure and compare its signing certificate, by exact DER certificate identity, with the certificate set published for that type in the OJA;
    • validate the LoTE structure, requested type, publication location, freshness, and sequence number.

    Trust-Source Dispatch¶

    Inputs and Outcome

    Inputs are the requested artifact or attestation and provider kind, provider certificate and service identity, required service type, territory CC (uppercase ISO 3166-1 Alpha-2), and current runtime validation time. Authentication, runtime acceptance and service eligibility are separate results. Runtime rejection SHALL return no Trust Anchor.

    The validator SHALL route QEAA requests exclusively through the pilot LOTL and TL, with no LoTE fallback. PID Providers, Wallet Providers, Providers of WRPAC, Providers of WRPRC, PuB-EAA Providers, EAA Providers, and Registrars and Registers SHALL use their respective seven retained JSON LoTE paths below. Stored lists and Trust Anchors SHALL preserve source/profile separation, following [ARF Trust Model, Section 6.6.3.6].

    Governing Sources

    The LoTE validation is governed by [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06)], with the pilot choices of Pilot LoTE Profile Constraints and List of Trusted Entities Validation. The pilot LOTL and TL validation is governed by [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1 (2026-05)] and [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08)], as applied in List of Trusted Lists Validation and Trusted List Validation.

    List of Trusted Lists Validation¶

    This process authenticates the current pilot LOTL for a Wallet Unit or WRP. The complete procedure in [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1 (2026-05), Clauses 4.0 and 4.1.1–4.1.4] SHALL govern, subject to the pilot bootstrap mapping below.

    Inputs and State

    The validator SHALL use the input and state contract of Clause 4.1.2, with the following substitutions for the preconfigured parameters of Clause 4.0:

    Pilot parameter Source parameter Pilot binding
    OJA-Loc OJEU-Loc Initially, the fixed locally configured URI of the pilot OJA publication.
    OJA-LOTL-Loc OJEU-LOTL-Loc Current LOTL XML location supplied by that publication.
    OJA-LOTL-Certs-Set OJEU-LOTL-Certs-Set Full set of LOTL-signing certificates supplied by that publication.

    The OJA publication is unsigned and is not independently authenticated. The validator SHALL accept its initial bootstrap data under the explicit APTITUDE discovery assumption at the fixed configured pilot OJA URI.

    Authentication

    The validator SHALL execute the complete referenced procedure, including its archived-pivot processing and authenticated parameter updates, using the mapped pilot parameters. Source-defined status and sub-status identifiers SHALL retain their original names, including identifiers containing OJEU.

    For LOTL and pivot signature authentication, the validator SHALL directly trust the list-signing certificate as specified in Clause 4.1.4, with authorization established by that procedure. The CA Trust Anchor Certificate profile and entity Sign/Seal certificate-chain rules SHALL NOT be applied to these directly trusted list-signing certificates; see Trust Anchor Certificate Profiles.

    Outputs

    The validator SHALL preserve Authenticated-LOTL, LOTL-Status and LOTL-Sub-Status as specified in Clauses 4.1.3–4.1.4, including initialization to void, all failure indications and supplementary indications, and the LOTL_VERIFICATION_PASSED success result. Authenticated-LOTL is the authenticated current XML LOTL on success and is void on authentication failure as specified by the source procedure.

    Trusted List Validation¶

    This process authenticates the pilot Trusted List for a Wallet Unit or WRP. The complete procedure in [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1, Clauses 4.2.1–4.2.4] SHALL govern, subject to the pilot bindings below.

    Input

    The validator SHALL use the input contract of Clause 4.2.2: CC is the uppercase ISO 3166-1 Alpha-2 country code of the emulated Member State whose TL is requested.

    Pilot Bindings

    The LOTL validation call in Clause 4.2.4 SHALL invoke List of Trusted Lists Validation, using its pilot OJA bootstrap mapping. From the resulting Authenticated-LOTL, the validator SHALL select the PointersToOtherTSL tuple whose scheme-territory qualifier equals CC and whose MIME-type qualifier identifies XML (application/vnd.etsi.tsl+xml), as defined in the LOTL profile. That tuple supplies both EUTL-Loc, the XML TL retrieval location, and EUTL-Certs-Set, the authorized TL-signing certificate set.

    The validator SHALL authenticate the TL signature using EUTL-Signer-Cert as a directly trusted certificate authorized by that tuple, as specified in Clause 4.2.4. These list-signing certificates are distinct from TL service digital identities and CA Trust Anchor Certificates. The CA Trust Anchor Certificate profile and entity Sign/Seal certificate-chain rules SHALL NOT be applied to the directly trusted TL-signing certificate; see Trust Anchor Certificate Profiles.

    Outputs

    The validator SHALL preserve Authenticated-EUTL, EUTL-Status and EUTL-Sub-Status under Clauses 4.2.3–4.2.4, including initialization to void, all failure and supplementary indications, and the EUTL_VERIFICATION_PASSED success result. Authenticated-EUTL is the authenticated XML TL on success and remains void on authentication failure under that procedure. If the TL's NextUpdate has passed, the procedure adds WARNING_EUTL_NEXTUPDATE_PASSED while retaining its source-defined authentication result.

    Pilot TL and LOTL Runtime Acceptance¶

    The dispatch procedure SHALL initiate required retrieval under the cache rules below and List of Trusted Lists Validation and Trusted List Validation, governed by [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1 (2026-05), Clauses 4.1–4.2]. Authentication hands off to the following common checks; these checks consume authentication outputs, retrieval time and persisted sequence/digest state and SHALL NOT invoke authentication again. Required refresh SHALL be attempted before rejecting a stale cached instance.

    1. Require successful authentication. Preserve all ETSI authentication statuses and sub-statuses on every local runtime failure. Report runtime acceptance separately; runtime rejection SHALL return no Trust Anchor or usable trust context.
    2. Independently check each authenticated current TL and LOTL against its applicable pilot profile in Trust Artifacts, requiring ListIssueDateTime not later than the current runtime validation time and NextUpdate later than that time, under [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08), Clauses 5.3.14–5.3.15]. An overdue TL SHALL be rejected even with EUTL_VERIFICATION_PASSED and WARNING_EUTL_NEXTUPDATE_PASSED.
    3. Apply the sequence and signed-content-digest conflict checks below independently to each current list. All profile, freshness and state checks SHALL succeed, and the highest accepted state SHALL be successfully persisted, before reporting runtime success or returning usable trust context. Failure to persist SHALL reject runtime use without a Trust Anchor. Dispatch SHALL require acceptance of both current lists.

    This process supplies authenticated, runtime-accepted TL service information and service identity or path context. The calling Sign/Seal Validation procedure owns QEAA matching, qualification and provider binding under [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1 (2026-05), Clauses 4.3 and 4.6]; matching alone does not establish qualification. Service digital identity, status and effective time follow [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08), Clauses 5.5.3–5.5.5]. Any applicable CA Trust Anchor follows the existing Trust Anchor definition; non-CA direct service identities remain governed by the cited matching procedures rather than the CA-anchor profile. Absent or ambiguous eligible identities SHALL yield no Trust Anchor.

    Current-List Cache and Rollback State

    For each current pilot TL and LOTL independently, the validator SHALL retrieve the current list when no accepted cached instance exists, 24 hours have elapsed since retrieval, or NextUpdate has been reached, whichever occurs first. A failed required refresh SHALL reject runtime use without a Trust Anchor. Publication and update detection follow [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08), Clause 6.1]; a published digest does not authenticate a list.

    For each logical current list, the validator SHALL persist across refreshes and restarts the highest accepted TSLSequenceNumber and a SHA-256 digest of the signed content (the canonicalized XML document after the enveloped-signature transform). Following [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1 (2025-08), Clause 5.3.2], it SHALL reject lower sequences and equal sequences with differing signed-content digests. It SHALL update this state only after all runtime checks succeed and before reporting runtime acceptance. State SHALL remain separate for each TL territory, the LOTL, and each retained LoTE type, preserving source/profile separation through authenticated location updates.

    Archived pivots SHALL follow the cited LOTL authentication procedure and SHALL be exempt from current-list cache, freshness and rollback checks. They SHALL NOT substitute for current lists in runtime Trust Anchor selection.

    List of Trusted Entities Validation¶

    This section defines the validation of a LoTE. The LoTE format is specified in List of Trusted Entities.

    APTITUDE Discovery Assumption

    The OJA publication is made available at a fixed, locally configured URI only available to the APTITUDE Partners, but is not signed or independently authenticated within APTITUDE.

    A Wallet Unit or WRP SHALL accept the type-specific LoTE location and authorized signing certificate set obtained from that publication.

    For the duration of APTITUDE, the OJA publication URI, each type-specific LoTE location, and the authorized signing certificate set for each type will remain unchanged. New versions of a LoTE SHALL replace the current version at that type's fixed location and SHALL be signed with a certificate from its fixed set.

  participant Distribution as LoTE Distribution Point

  Client->>OJA: Request discovery for required LoTE type
  OJA-->>Client: Return fixed location and signer certificate set
  Client->>Distribution: Request current LoTE at the type-specific location
  Distribution-->>Client: Return JSON/JWT LoTE

  Client->>Client: Compare signer certificate with OJA certificate set

  Client->>Client: Parse x5c chain, match subject of signing certificate to TEName and select service

  Client->>Client: Check SdiMatching, status applicability and critical extensions
  Client->>Client: Persist highest accepted sequence and digest
  Client->>Client: Accept LoTE and return the Trust Anchor

    List of Trusted Entities Validation Process¶

    Clause references in this process are to [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06)] (work in progress) and are given with each step. Where the pilot adds a rule, the step says so.

    Input Variables:

    • Requested-LoTE-Type: The LoTE type required for the artifact or Attestation being validated. The constraints of that type are those of the table for it in Pilot LoTE Profile Constraints. The type is known in advance to the caller or is determined by the caller from the qcStatements extension of C; this specification defines no mapping from that extension to a type.
    • Sign-Seal-Chain: The x5c certificate chain [C, I_1, ..., I_n] of the artifact or Attestation being validated, where C is the sign/seal certificate that signed it and I_n is the last intermediate certificate. When the chain has no intermediate certificate, n = 0 and the chain is [C]. The entity is identified from C in step 6, so no separate entity identity is an input.
    • Requested-Service-Type: The ServiceTypeIdentifier required for that artifact or protocol.
    • OJA-Loc: The fixed, locally configured URI of the OJA publication.
    • Highest-Accepted-Sequence: The highest LoTESequenceNumber previously accepted for Requested-LoTE-Type, if any, together with a SHA-256 digest of the accepted signed list content at that sequence number; this state is retained across refreshes and restarts. The list content is the JWS Payload octets.
    • Date-Time: The runtime validation time. It is used by the freshness checks. It is supplied as the Date-Time input of the status applicability check only when StatusStartingTimePresent of Requested-LoTE-Type is TRUE ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.5.2], INP-4.5.2-01).

    Advisory on LoTE Selection

    This note is non-normative. A verifier may use the qcStatements extension of the sign/seal certificate C to determine which LoTE to fetch.

    Working and Output Variables:

    • LoTE: The JSON/JWT LoTE being processed.
    • OJA-LoTE-Loc: The fixed location published by the OJA for Requested-LoTE-Type.
    • OJA-LoTE-Certs-Set: The set of certificates published by the OJA for verifying Requested-LoTE-Type.
    • LoTE-Format: The format of LoTE, JSON.
    • LoTE-Signer-Cert: The certificate used to verify the signature or seal on LoTE.
    • Status: PROCESS_PASSED or PROCESS_FAILED, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.1].
    • Sub-Status: The sub-status supplementing Status. Each failing step sets it to the value named in that step. The values are TRY_LATER, LOTE_SIGNER_NOT_AUTHENTICATED, LOTE_EXPIRED, LOTE_NOT_YET_VALID, NO_MATCHING_TE_SERVICE, STATUS_NOT_APPLICABLE, STATUS_CONFLICT, SDI_CONSTRAINT_FAILURE and CRITICAL_EXTENSION_FAILURE ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.1.2]), and the pilot values LOTE_OJA_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND, LOTE_SCHEMA_INVALID, LOTE_TYPE_MISMATCH, LOTE_SIGNER_CERT_NOT_VALID, LOTE_LOCATION_MISMATCH, LOTE_PROFILE_VIOLATION, LOTE_SEQUENCE_ROLLBACK, LOTE_PUBLICATION_CONFLICT and LOTE_STATE_NOT_PERSISTED. Where the failing check is the cryptographic verification of the signature, the sub-status is the sub-indication returned by that verification ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2.4], PRO-4.2.4-06).
    • Current-LoTE-Certs-Set: The set of LoTE-signing certificates used to authenticate the requested LoTE at current time. It is set to OJA-LoTE-Certs-Set ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2.4], PRO-4.2.4-02).
    • Current-LoTE-Loc: The URI from which the requested LoTE is retrieved at current time. It is set to OJA-LoTE-Loc (PRO-4.2.4-02).
    • Authenticated-LoTE: The authenticated LoTE. It is set only when Status is PROCESS_PASSED, so that no content of the list is used before every step has succeeded.
    • Trust-Anchor: The selected Trust Anchor (A_i, step 7). It is set only when Status is PROCESS_PASSED.

    All outputs are initialized to void ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2.3], OUT-4.2.3-01). A runtime rejection returns no Trust Anchor.

    JSON LoTE Signature Verification¶

    This procedure applies when the LoTE is JSON formatted and uses the Compact JAdES Baseline B profile.

    The validator SHALL perform the following operations. Values from the payload SHALL NOT be used to extract Trust Anchors before all validation operations succeed:

    1. Parse the JWS Compact Serialization into exactly three parts and reject invalid Base64url encoding, duplicate JSON member names, detached or unencoded payloads, and unsupported critical header parameters. The protected header SHALL contain alg with value ES256, an integer iat, and x5t#S256; x5c, kid, x5u, and other certificate-binding parameters SHALL NOT be used. The iat value is a claimed signing time, not a trusted timestamp. Failure: LOTE_SCHEMA_INVALID.
    2. Decode x5t#S256 from Base64url and select exactly one certificate from OJA-LoTE-Certs-Set whose DER encoding has that SHA-256 digest. Set it as LoTE-Signer-Cert. If no certificate or more than one has that digest, the sub-status is LOTE_SIGNER_NOT_AUTHENTICATED.
    3. Verify the JWS signature over the JWS Signing Input using the P-256 public key in LoTE-Signer-Cert and the ES256 algorithm.
    4. Decode the JWT Claims Set and require the private LoTE claim containing the list object. Validate that object against the applicable profiled JSON payload schema. Failure: LOTE_SCHEMA_INVALID.
    5. Confirm that the LoTEType in the signature-verified payload equals Requested-LoTE-Type. Failure: LOTE_TYPE_MISMATCH.

    If any operation fails, validation SHALL stop with Status set to PROCESS_FAILED and Sub-Status set to the value given for that operation.

    LoTE Validation Operations¶

    The validator SHALL perform the following steps in order. A failing step SHALL stop the process with Status set to PROCESS_FAILED and Sub-Status set to the value named in that step.

    1. Retrieve the current list. [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2.4], PRO-4.2.4-01 and PRO-4.2.4-02.

      • Select exactly one Requested-LoTE-Type entry from the trusted OJA publication at OJA-Loc and require a non-empty authorized signing certificate set.
      • Obtain OJA-LoTE-Loc and OJA-LoTE-Certs-Set for that type, and set Current-LoTE-Certs-Set and Current-LoTE-Loc to them. Download the current LoTE from OJA-LoTE-Loc when no accepted cached list exists, 24 hours have elapsed since the cached list was retrieved, or its NextUpdate has been reached, whichever occurs first. A cached list SHALL NOT be used after a required refresh fails.

      The validator SHALL NOT use the location or certificate set of another LoTE type.

      If the type-specific entry is absent or its certificate set is empty, the sub-status is LOTE_OJA_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND. If more than one entry exists for the type, the sub-status is LOTE_OJA_ENTRY_NOT_FOUND. If no file can be retrieved from OJA-LoTE-Loc, or a required refresh fails, the sub-status is TRY_LATER (PRO-4.2.4-01).

    2. Verify signature, schema, and type.

      Require JSON format; a list that is not JSON fails with LOTE_SCHEMA_INVALID. Run the JSON LoTE Signature Verification above. Its failures set the sub-status given there.

    3. Match the signing certificate.

      Require LoTE-Signer-Cert to match a certificate in OJA-LoTE-Certs-Set by exact DER certificate identity; otherwise the sub-status is LOTE_SIGNER_NOT_AUTHENTICATED. Require its X.509 validity period to include the validation time; otherwise the sub-status is LOTE_SIGNER_CERT_NOT_VALID.

    4. Check the scheme URI and publication location. Failure: LOTE_LOCATION_MISMATCH.

      • Require SchemeInformationURI to contain exactly one URI, equal to OJA-Loc, and require DistributionPoints to be absent. The current list location is OJA-LoTE-Loc.
      • For all LoTE types, require PointersToOtherLoTE to be absent.

    5. Check profile, freshness, and sequence. [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2.4], PRO-4.2.4-07 and PRO-4.2.4-08; the profile, freshness and sequence checks of this step are pilot checks in addition to those two requirements.

      • Require LoTEVersionIdentifier to be 1, a positive integer LoTESequenceNumber, and the type-specific values and presence rules of the Pilot LoTE Profile Constraints and of the LoTE topic for Requested-LoTE-Type, including the service type identifiers and the service status rules. For a PuB-EAA Provider service, require StatusStartingTime not to be later than the validation time. The absence of TrustedEntitiesList is not a failure of this step; step 6 then finds no matching service. Failure: LOTE_PROFILE_VIOLATION.
      • Require a valid ListIssueDateTime and NextUpdate, with NextUpdate a date-time value that is not null, the issue time not later than the validation time, and the interval between them no longer than six months. Failure of this requirement: LOTE_PROFILE_VIOLATION.
      • If the NextUpdate is not later than the validation time, the sub-status is LOTE_EXPIRED. Otherwise, if the ListIssueDateTime is later than the validation time, the sub-status is LOTE_NOT_YET_VALID.
      • If Highest-Accepted-Sequence exists, reject a LoTESequenceNumber below it: LOTE_SEQUENCE_ROLLBACK.
      • If the sequence number is equal but the digest of the signed list content differs from the previously accepted digest, reject the list as a publication conflict: LOTE_PUBLICATION_CONFLICT.

    6. Identify the entity and the service. This is an APTITUDE choice that extends [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.4.4] for piloting purposes.

      • Parse Sign-Seal-Chain and take C as its first certificate. If the chain cannot be parsed or is empty, the sub-status is NO_MATCHING_TE_SERVICE.
      • Match the subject name of C to a TEName value of a TrustedEntityInformation of the list. Optionally, also match the organizationIdentifier of the subject of C to a TETradeName value.
      • From the matched entity, select the TrustedEntityService whose ServiceTypeIdentifier equals Requested-Service-Type and which is present in the list. ServiceHistory SHALL NOT supply the service.
      • If no entity or no service matches, the sub-status is NO_MATCHING_TE_SERVICE.

    7. Select the Trust Anchor. This is a pilot rule, applied to the certificate chain that the caller supplies.

      • Obtain the certificates [A_1, ..., A_m] from the ServiceDigitalIdentity of the selected service. ServiceDigitalIdentity is REQUIRED, so an empty set fails with LOTE_PROFILE_VIOLATION.
      • When n = 0, take the issuer name and authority key identifier of C; otherwise take those of I_n. Select the A_i whose subject name equals that issuer name and whose subject key identifier equals that authority key identifier. If no A_i matches, the sub-status is NO_MATCHING_TE_SERVICE. If more than one A_i matches, this is not a failure: the matching certificates share one public key and subject name under [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1 (2025-11), Annex H.3], and any one of them is selected as A_i.

    8. Check SdiMatching. Apply the SdiMatching constraint of Requested-LoTE-Type, together with the per-certificate rule of the LoTE topic, to the ServiceDigitalIdentity of the selected service. If it is not met, the sub-status is SDI_CONSTRAINT_FAILURE ([ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.6.4], PRO-4.6.4-08).

    9. Determine the status applicability. This step composes [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.5] directly. TE-Service-Entries is the selected service. ServiceHistory never supplies trust, because no Historical-Information-Period is supplied.

      • For a PuB-EAA Provider, Target-TE-Service-Statuses is the notified status URI of this type, Date-Time is the runtime validation time, and StatusStartingTimePresent is TRUE. The status found is the ServiceStatus when Date-Time is at or after StatusStartingTime (PRO-4.5.4-04 i). If no status is found, the sub-status is STATUS_NOT_APPLICABLE.
      • For the other six types, including the EAA Provider, no target is supplied, StatusStartingTimePresent is FALSE and no Date-Time is supplied. The EAA list carries no ServiceStatus and no StatusStartingTime, and the EAA interpretation is written here in full because [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Annex C] has none. The status found is the implicit status http://uri.etsi.org/19605/StatusDetn/ImplicitStatus/Applicable of Annex A (PRO-4.5.4-03 i a), so the check passes.
      • If more than one status is found, PRO-4.5.4-06 and PRO-4.5.4-07 apply. StatusCoherenceOnSti is TRUE for every type, so a conflict sets the sub-status to STATUS_CONFLICT.
      • If the status found does not match the target, the sub-status is STATUS_NOT_APPLICABLE (PRO-4.5.4-05 ii).

    10. Check critical extensions. [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.6.4], PRO-4.6.4-13 and PRO-4.6.4-14. Process all critical extensions present in the signature-verified list. The only recognized critical extension is the Wallet Provider ServiceUniqueIdentifier in ServiceInformationExtensions. A critical extension that is not recognized, or whose associated check fails, sets the sub-status to CRITICAL_EXTENSION_FAILURE.

    11. Persist the state and accept. Persist Highest-Accepted-Sequence and the digest of the accepted signed list content for Requested-LoTE-Type, after steps 1 to 10 have all succeeded and before success is returned. If this state cannot be persisted, the sub-status is LOTE_STATE_NOT_PERSISTED. Otherwise set Authenticated-LoTE to LoTE, set Trust-Anchor to the selected A_i, and set Status to PROCESS_PASSED.

    Remarks

    • The OJA page is the discovery source within the APTITUDE ecosystem. A passed validation result establishes signature integrity and conformity with the certificate set and location supplied by that page, subject to the assumption stated above. It also establishes that Trust-Anchor is taken from the service that applies at the validation time, of the entity identified by C.
    • PROCESS_PASSED means that the LoTE is valid and the Trust Anchor is selected; it does not mean that the certificate path is valid. The calling process runs the X.509 Certificate Chain Validation with the returned Trust Anchor.
    • The JSON x5t#S256 value identifies the signing certificate in the type-specific OJA set.
    • Cached-list reuse is governed by LoTE Validation Operations, including required refresh, signer validity and persistent rollback state.
    • This process composes [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.2] and [Clause 4.5] directly. It does not run [Clause 4.6] as a whole: steps 8 and 10 apply its PRO-4.6.4-08, PRO-4.6.4-13 and PRO-4.6.4-14, and steps 6 and 7 replace its certificate-based search of [Clause 4.4]. Clause 4.6 would require POE-Set and Cert-VD whenever Date-Time is given (INP-4.6.2-01), and the pilot does not use past validation. Pivot processing (PRO-4.2.4-03) and the list-of-lists process of Clause 4.3 are omitted.
    • LOTE_EXPIRED and LOTE_NOT_YET_VALID are the sub-statuses used for a LoTE. The LOLOTE_* names of [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clause 4.1.2] apply to lists of lists, which are not used.
