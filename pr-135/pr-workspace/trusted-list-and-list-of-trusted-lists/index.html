    Trusted list and list of trusted lists

    Trusted List¶

    The pilot Trusted List is the XML trust source for QEAA services, as envisaged in [ARF Trust Model, Section 6.3.2.4]. [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1, Clauses 5.1–5.7, Annexes B–E and G] governs its data model.

    APTITUDE Implementation Choice

    The TL SHALL use XML encoded in UTF-8, TSLVersionIdentifier = 6, TSLType = http://uri.etsi.org/TrstSvc/TrustedList/TSLType/EUgeneric, and SchemeTerritory identifying the emulated Member State (CC). WP2 SHALL operate the pilot scheme as specified in Trust Architecture.

    Runtime-Consumed Fields¶

    Presence and interpretation follow the cited clauses of [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1]. This table identifies the fields used by the pilot; it does not replace the complete model or its other mandatory fields.

    Field Presence Pilot role and source clause
    TSLVersionIdentifier, TSLType, SchemeTerritory REQUIRED Values specified above; Clauses 5.3.1, 5.3.3, 5.3.10.
    SchemeOperatorName, SchemeOperatorAddress, SchemeName REQUIRED SHALL identify the WP2 pilot operator and scheme; Clauses 5.3.4–5.3.6.
    PolicyOrLegalNotice REQUIRED SHALL identify pilot emulation and the absence of institutional EU qualified status; Clause 5.3.11.
    TSLSequenceNumber REQUIRED Version sequence with source-defined increment semantics; Clause 5.3.2.
    SchemeInformationURI REQUIRED Pilot scheme information; Clause 5.3.7.
    PointersToOtherTSL REQUIRED for the emulated EU Member State profile SHALL include the pilot LOTL pointer with location; Clause 5.3.13.
    ListIssueDateTime, NextUpdate REQUIRED Issue and latest update times; Clauses 5.3.14–5.3.15.
    HistoricalInformationPeriod REQUIRED 65535; Clause 5.3.12.
    DistributionPoints OPTIONAL Current-list locations; Clause 5.3.16.
    TSPName, TSPTradeName REQUIRED Provider name and registration identifier/trade names, including the condition on availability of an official registration identifier; Clauses 5.4.1–5.4.2.
    ServiceTypeIdentifier REQUIRED SHALL be http://uri.etsi.org/TrstSvc/Svctype/EAA/Q for QEAA services; Clause 5.5.1.1(k).
    ServiceDigitalIdentity REQUIRED Listed service identity; Clause 5.5.3.
    ServiceStatus, StatusStartingTime REQUIRED Current status and its effective start; Clauses 5.5.4–5.5.5.
    ServiceHistory CONDITIONAL Present only when history applies; absent where no prior history exists. Instances follow Clauses 5.5.10–5.6.
    ServiceInformationExtensions OPTIONAL, subject to applicable source conditions Presence, criticality and interpretation follow Clauses 5.5.9, 5.6.6 and Annexes D and G.

    QEAA qualification and binding to the listed provider SHALL be evaluated by the Sign/Seal Validation procedure. Runtime acceptance, including rejection of overdue TLs while preserving the authentication result, is specified in Trust Anchor Validation.

    Signature and Publication¶

    The TL SHALL carry the scheme operator's XAdES-B-B signature and satisfy [ETSI TS 119 612, Clause 5.7 and Annex B], including its algorithm and list-signer certificate restrictions. The protected ds:KeyInfo contains the TLSO signing certificate without an associated certificate chain. XML retrieval and publication SHALL follow [ETSI TS 119 612, Clauses 6.1–6.2], using application/vnd.etsi.tsl+xml. The version-specific schema is the Annex C attachment ts_119612v020401p0.zip for V2.4.1.

    List Of Trusted Lists¶

    The pilot LOTL is the signed pointer list through which the pilot TL locations and authorized TL-signing identities are published. [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1, Clauses 5.3.3, 5.3.7, 5.3.13–5.3.18, 5.7, 6 and Annexes A–D] governs its structure, signature and publication except for the pilot substitutions below. Authentication metadata and pivot information follow [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1, Clauses 4.0–4.1].

    APTITUDE Implementation Choice

    WP2 SHALL operate the sole pilot signed pointer-list profile as the LOTL scheme operator identified in Trust Architecture. The LOTL SHALL use XML encoded in UTF-8, TSLVersionIdentifier = 6, TSLType = http://uri.etsi.org/TrstSvc/TrustedList/TSLType/EUlistofthelists, and SchemeTerritory = EU.

    LOTL Profile Metadata¶

    The source model and its presence rules apply; the following table specifies the pilot bindings rather than reproducing that model.

    Field Pilot binding and source clause
    SchemeOperatorName, SchemeOperatorAddress, SchemeName, PolicyOrLegalNotice SHALL identify the WP2 operator and pilot scheme, with a legal notice identifying pilot emulation and the absence of institutional EU qualified status. ETSI TS 119 612, Clauses 5.3.4–5.3.6 and 5.3.11.
    TSLSequenceNumber SHALL carry the list sequence number with source-defined increment semantics; Clause 5.3.2.
    SchemeInformationURI SHALL provide scheme information and the applicable OJA and archived pivot references, retaining the source order and transition information under the OJA substitution below; Clause 5.3.7 and ETSI TS 119 615, Clause 4.1.
    ListIssueDateTime, NextUpdate SHALL carry issue and latest-update metadata under Clauses 5.3.14–5.3.15.
    PointersToOtherTSL SHALL include the self-pointer as the first tuple and a pointer to each pilot TL. Each tuple SHALL bind TSLLocation, authorized signing identities in ServiceDigitalIdentities, and the TSLType, SchemeTerritory and MIME-type qualifiers under Clause 5.3.13 and Annex D. The self-pointer identifies the pilot LOTL with territory EU; each TL pointer identifies its emulated territory CC. XML pointers use application/vnd.etsi.tsl+xml.
    TrustServiceProviderList SHALL be absent: this pointer-only artefact contains no TSP/service list; Clause 5.3.18.

    The self-pointer and applicable archived pivot references SHALL preserve the source-defined signer/location continuity information of [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1, Clause 4.1]. The LOTL signature and publication SHALL follow [ETSI TS 119 612 V2.4.1, Clauses 5.7 and 6, Annexes A–D], using the version-specific Annex C schema attachment ts_119612v020401p0.zip.

    Pilot OJA Bootstrap¶

    The pilot substitutes the following parameters for those in [ETSI TS 119 615 V1.4.1, Clause 4.0]:

    Pilot parameter Source parameter Pilot meaning
    OJA-Loc OJEU-Loc Fixed configured URI of the pilot OJA publication.
    OJA-LOTL-Loc OJEU-LOTL-Loc Initial current XML LOTL location supplied by that publication.
    OJA-LOTL-Certs-Set OJEU-LOTL-Certs-Set Full initial authorized LOTL-signing certificate set supplied by that publication.

    The OJA publication is unsigned and is not independently authenticated. Consumers SHALL accept its bootstrap data under the explicit APTITUDE discovery assumption at the fixed configured OJA URI.

    List of Trusted Entities¶

    This section describes the format and contents of the LoTE and how it is used to provide Trust Anchors within APTITUDE.

    The LoTE takes the form of a list of entities that have been granted a particular status under an approval scheme [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clause 4.1]. Each retained LoTE lists the trusted entities of one of the following seven types. Six of them are EU types registered in [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clause C.2.1]; the EAA type is APTITUDE-specific:

    1. PID Providers;
    2. Wallet Providers;
    3. Providers of WRPAC;
    4. Providers of WRPRC;
    5. PuB-EAA Providers;
    6. Registrars and Registers;
    7. EAA Providers.

    In the context of the EUDI Wallet, the EC will publish the related LoTE on the eIDAS Dashboard, which will be accessible at https://eidas.ec.europa.eu/efda/wallet.

    Each LoTE consists of a ListAndSchemeInformation component with explicit scheme information, followed by a TrustedEntitiesList component, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clauses 5 and 6]. The general principles of [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clause 6.1] apply by reference. The profile is bound to [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1] and to its JSON binding at tag v1.1.1, as stated in Data Models. Every retained LoTE has SchemeTerritory EU and uses JSON only; no XML binding applies.

    The LoTE Provider is the List of Trusted Entities Scheme Operator (LoTESO) of [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clauses 4.5 and 6.3.4]; this specification uses the name LoTE Provider for that role.

    All retained LoTE SHALL use JSON and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].

    APTITUDE Implementation Choices

    • EAA Providers SHALL have a dedicated LoTE.
    • Each LoTE type SHALL be published at a distinct endpoint. A LoTE consumer SHALL use the endpoint published for the required LoTE type and SHALL NOT infer the endpoint of one LoTE type from that of another.
    • The Official Journal of APTITUDE (OJA) SHALL publish a fixed location and fixed authorized signing certificate set for each LoTE type. The publication URI, type-specific locations, and certificate sets SHALL remain unchanged during APTITUDE.

    APTITUDE OJA Publication

    The OJA publication SHALL remain at a fixed URI for the duration of APTITUDE. It is not signed or independently authenticated. LoTE consumers accept its type-specific locations and signing certificate sets as APTITUDE discovery data. A successful comparison with that certificate set does not independently authenticate the OJA publication.

    Runtime acceptance, caching, refresh failure and persistent rollback state are specified in Trust Anchor Validation. QEAA trust uses the pilot TL and LOTL profiles above, following [ARF Trust Model, Section 6.3.2.4].

    Data Models¶

    This section specifies the profiles and formats that the various LoTE within the APTITUDE ecosystem SHALL utilize, depending on their specific use cases.

    The following repository provides the base normative JSON schema of [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1, Clause A.1], at tag v1.1.1: https://forge.etsi.org/rep/esi/x19_60201_lists_of_trusted_entities. If the schema conflicts with the text of [ETSI TS 119 602 V1.1.1], the text prevails, as stated in Clause A.1.

    The LoTE OpenAPI description documents the APTITUDE type-specific retrieval endpoints and the JSON LoTE carried in a signed compact JWT. Its paths and example values are illustrative; the actual fixed endpoint and authorized signing certificates for each type are published by the OJA. The OpenAPI document covers the seven retained JSON LoTE types. XML TL and LOTL publication is specified in their sibling profiles above.

    The following formats SHALL be used for the different types of lists:

    • Wallet Provider Lists are published in JSON format, and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].

    • Provider of WRPRC Lists are published in JSON format, and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].
    • Registrar and Register Provider Lists are published in JSON format, and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].
    • PID Provider Lists are published in JSON format, and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].
    • EAA Provider Lists are published in JSON format, and SHALL be signed with Compact JAdES Baseline B signature, as defined in [ETSI TS 119 182-1].
    Compact JAdES Baseline B Signature Profile¶

    APTITUDE Implementation Choice

    The use of a JWT representation for a JSON-formatted LoTE and the selection of x5t#S256 as the certificate-binding parameter are implementation choices. The JWT Claims Set SHALL contain the LoTE object as the private LoTE claim.

    For a JSON-formatted LoTE, the Compact JAdES Baseline B signature SHALL be used, its protected JOSE header SHALL satisfy [ETSI TS 119 182-1, Clauses 5.1 and 6.3]:

    Parameter Presence Requirement
    alg REQUIRED Identifies the signature algorithm. It SHALL be ES256.
    iat REQUIRED Specifies the claimed signing time.
    x5t#S256 REQUIRED Contains the Base64url-encoded SHA-256 thumbprint of the DER encoding of the authorized signing certificate in the type-specific OJA certificate set.

    The x5c, kid, and x5u header parameters SHALL NOT be used in the APTITUDE profile. A validator SHALL select the signing certificate only from the type-specific OJA certificate set, using x5t#S256.

    APTITUDE LoTE format

    Media type of the published LoTE SHALL be application/jwt, i.e., the LoTE SHALL be a JWT carrying the signed LoTE (see the Compact JAdES Baseline B profile below). [ETSI TS 119 605 V0.0.3 (2026-06), Clauses C.1.2 and C.2.2] sets application/jose for the PID and Wallet Provider lists; the APTITUDE value is application/jwt for simplicity.

    APTITUDE History and Publication

    • APTITUDE WP2 SHALL publish only the current LoTE version for each type at its fixed endpoint.

    • For types other than PuB-EAA Provider, the current list SHALL NOT contain historical service information, and the LoTE Provider SHALL NOT publish earlier instances for historical membership validation.

    These APTITUDE choices depart from the archive retrieval URI and self-pointer requirements for the corresponding profiles in [ETSI TS 119 602, Annexes D-G and I]. The PuB-EAA Provider LoTE SHALL retain its required ServiceHistory in the current list.

    APTITUDE Implementation Choices

    The Trust Anchors for the Issuance and Revocation services SHALL be the same, and SHALL be listed in the ServiceDigitalIdentity component of the ServiceInformation component.

    For the ETSI-profiled list types other than Registrar, the ServiceTypeIdentifier SHALL be formed as a URI string http://uri.etsi.org/19602/SvcType/{Specific_attestation}/Issuance or http://uri.etsi.org/19602/SvcType/{Specific_attestation}/Revocation. The {specific_attestation} fragment SHALL be valued as:

    • PID for PID Providers Trust Anchors;
    • WalletSolution for Wallet Providers' owned Wallet Solution Trust Anchors;
    • WRPAC for Providers of WRPAC's Trust Anchors;
    • WRPRC for Provider of WRPRC's Trust Anchors;
    • PubEAA for Pub-EAA Providers' Trust Anchors.

    For the EAA Provider lists within APTITUDE, ServiceTypeIdentifier SHALL use http://aptitude-consortium.github.io/SvcType/{Specific_attestation}/Issuance, http://aptitude-consortium.github.io/SvcType/{Specific_attestation}/Revocation as applicable.

    For the Registrar LoTE type, the ServiceTypeIdentifier SHALL be formed as a URI string http://uri.etsi.org/19602/SvcType/Register.
